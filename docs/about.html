ABOUT US

The story behind Zygote Digital Promoters

Shahjahanpur's first and leading digital marketing agency.

Founder

Shashwat Mishra

Shashwat Mishra is a 24-year-old software engineer and digital marketer with over 6 years of experience in the field. He has worked directly in the political domain, delivering campaign and content strategy for candidates from the Indian National Congress, BJP, and Rashtriya Janata Dal across Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, and Haryana — creating quality content that helped these campaigns reach far more people than traditional outreach alone.

That experience running high-stakes, fast-moving campaigns shaped how Zygote Digital Promoters works today: data-driven, deadline-aware, and focused on content that actually performs, not just content that looks nice.

Why Zygote?

After years of freelance digital marketing work, Shashwat brought that experience home to Shahjahanpur, building Zygote Digital Promoters into a full digital marketing agency and BPO — now trusted by over 1,000 clients across healthcare, education, politics, and local business.
